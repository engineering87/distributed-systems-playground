# Code of conduct

This project is used in classrooms, so it welcomes people who are just starting out as much as experts.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with questions that seem basic.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Keep discussions on topic and in public, so that others can learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, including in code review.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Repeatedly pushing a discussion after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who break them repeatedly.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through the email address on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/engineering87). Reports are kept confidential.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
